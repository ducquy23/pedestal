How Maersk turned decades of customs expertise into AI that serves its customers

Maersk is using AI to give its customers faster, more reliable answers on their customs data, built on the knowledge of its trade and compliance experts.

  • Expert-grounded

  • Validated against Maersk data

  • Answers in seconds

The problem

Customs is one of the most complex parts of global trade. For businesses managing thousands of shipments, getting timely insights into customs operations can have significant financial and operational consequences. Yet accessing those insights has traditionally been slow and required specialist knowledge and manual effort. Maersk wanted to change that, making critical information faster and easier to access for its teams and customers.

The approach

Maersk partnered with Pedestal to capture the expertise of its customs specialists in a governed knowledge layer, validated against Maersk’s own data. Maersk’s experts stay in the loop, so the knowledge keeps improving.

In production

Maersk’s customs teams use the solution to answer customer questions faster and prepare customer reviews with less manual effort, with high accuracy, validated against Maersk’s own data. Maersk has begun extending access to selected customers.

The results

Maersk’s teams spend less time searching and reconciling data and more time with their customers, enabling more proactive conversations.
