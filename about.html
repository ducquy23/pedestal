AI agents that understand how your business runs

Building agents has become ridiculously easy, and almost anyone can do it today. However, impact at enterprise scale requires connecting to enterprise data, and that’s the hard part. Enterprise data lives in multiple places, has different meanings and definitions, and is constantly changing.

Pedestal works with your existing data by building a layer on top of it (in your cloud) to make sense of it. We scan your business to build a “Logistics Graph,” and it talks to your employees to fill in gaps and make corrections.

Leading retail, manufacturing and shipping companies use Pedestal today. Their agents coordinate deliveries, process orders into ERP systems and provide global shipping insights.

Who we are

Raj Aggarwal (CEO) and Henry Cipolla (CTO) founded Pedestal. It is their third company together, after Localytics (which continues to operate and grow) and Demand Sage (acquired by Snap). Before Pedestal, Raj was General Manager of Generative AI at AWS.

Pedestal is based in Boston and is backed by Eclipse, RRE, Converge VC, Timescale VC and VitalStage Ventures. Read about the seed round on TechCrunch (opens in a new tab).
