Labor optimization

Tomorrow’s staffing, planned, approved, and in your WMS before the shift.

The agent compares tomorrow’s work with who is scheduled and recommends the moves that close the gap. Then it keeps going: it asks the shift manager to approve in Slack and writes the approved plan back to your WMS.

Predictive maintenance

Catches equipment before it fails, and calls the captain when it can’t wait.

The agent watches sensor readings and maintenance records across the fleet, predicts what will fail and when, and escalates to match: a work order for routine fixes, a Slack message to the terminal team, and a phone call to the ship’s captain when cargo is at risk.
